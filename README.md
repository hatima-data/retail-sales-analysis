# reatail-sales

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. Sales & Stock Balance:
   - Total Units Sold: 201M units.
   - Total Stock Remaining: 40M units.
   - High inventory levels in specific categories (e.g., Electronics & Appliances) indicate potential overstocking, leading to increased warehousing costs.

2. Warranty Claims Distribution:
   - Total Warranties: 1M claims.
   - Certain product brands exhibit higher warranty request rates, highlighting potential manufacturing or quality control issues.

3. Customer Payment Preferences:
   - Customers predominantly prefer Credit/Debit Cards and Cash on Delivery (COD). Digital wallet adoption (UPI) shows steady growth potential.

4. Category & Pricing Performance:
   - Appliances and Electronics generate the highest revenue streams, making them core drivers of total turnover.
     
## 🎯 Strategic Recommendations

* Inventory Optimization: Implement promotional discounts or clearance strategies for high-stock items (40M remaining) to free up warehouse capacity.
* Vendor & Quality Management: Review contracts and quality assurance protocols for brands associated with the highest volume of warranty claims (1M claims).
* Payment Gateways Integration: Incentivize digital payment adoption to streamline checkouts and minimize manual handling fees associated with COD.
